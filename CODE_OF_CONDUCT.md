# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to be respectful, specific, and willing to revise claims when evidence changes. Make room for different backgrounds and levels of familiarity, and focus critique on the code, documentation, or decision.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publishing private information, and deliberate exposure of credentials or private runtime data. Do not use the project to target another person or system.

Report a concern privately through the repository host's reporting channel or to the maintainer. Do not include secrets or sensitive logs in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in confidence where the available channel permits, and retaliation for a good-faith report is not acceptable.
